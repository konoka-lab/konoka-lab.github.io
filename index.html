03 / At origin

at origin.

Colombia · May–July 2023

Coffee Farm Experience in Quindio, Colombia

Living and volunteering on a Colombian coffee farm brought the work behind every cup into focus.

  • Worked alongside farmers across cultivation, harvesting, processing, and quality control.
  • Connected with three local specialty coffee shops to support collaboration within the community.
  • Shared knowledge through coffee events and workshops with students from Colombia’s coffee academy.

This experience at origin helped shape an approach to producer relationships, sourcing, and sustainability.

04 / Professional

Coffee, in practice.

Sourcing, quality & collaboration

2023–2026

Green Buying Assistant

Onyx Coffee Lab

Connecting producers, coffee quality, and transparent sourcing.

  • Producer relationships & quality. Build long-term, trust-based relationships with producers; evaluate green coffee using SCAA cupping protocols and share feedback to support ethical, transparent sourcing.
  • From origin to roastery. Manage international logistics for traceability and efficiency. Operate Diedrich 35 kg and Stronghold S7 roasters, developing roast profiles and conducting quality control.
  • Transparency & impact. Build an Airtable purchasing database for Onyx’s transparency report and B Corp certification work, while contributing sourcing data for social and environmental initiatives.
  • Public coffee education. Host public cuppings and talks to share specialty coffee knowledge and Onyx’s sourcing philosophy.
